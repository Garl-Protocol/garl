# GARL Protocol Trademark Policy

_Last updated: 2026-04-15_

The names **"GARL"**, **"GARL Protocol"**, **"GARL Receipt"**, **"GARL Verified"**, the GARL wordmark, and the GARL identicon-style logo (collectively, the _"GARL Marks"_) identify the open protocol and its **canonical registry** operated at `https://api.garl.ai`. The Apache 2.0 license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the source code — it does **not** grant a license to the GARL Marks.

This document describes the community practices for using the GARL Marks. It is a trademark policy, not a legal grant; all rights are reserved.

## 1. Permitted, no permission needed

- **Referring to the project.** You may say "built with GARL", "supports GARL Protocol", "integrates the GARL Receipt Action", or similar factual references in documentation, blog posts, talks, and README files.
- **Forks and derivatives** that remain nominative (e.g. "my-org's GARL fork", "GARL-compatible"), provided you do **not** claim to be "the GARL registry" or "GARL Verified".
- **Educational, research, and journalistic use.**
- **Using the unmodified GARL Verified badge SVG** served from `api.garl.ai/api/v1/badge/...` in READMEs and dashboards. The badge content is generated by the canonical registry; its presence is an assertion about canonical registry state.

## 2. Permission required

You must obtain written permission (open a GitHub issue or email `contact@garl.ai`) before:

- Running a hosted service and calling it "GARL", "GARL Registry", "GARL Verified Registry", or any name a reasonable user could confuse with the canonical registry.
- Publishing a package to npm / PyPI / GitHub Marketplace under the `@garl-protocol/*`, `garl-protocol`, or `Garl-Protocol/*` namespaces.
- Selling merchandise, training courses, or certifications that imply official endorsement.
- Using the GARL wordmark or logo in a product UI in a way that could suggest the product is the canonical GARL registry or is certified by it.

## 3. Not permitted

- Claiming that receipts issued by your own fork or self-hosted deployment are "GARL Verified" in the sense that the canonical registry has verified them. The **GARL Verified** status is a property of the canonical registry only.
- Altering the GARL logo so it could be mistaken for the official mark.
- Using the GARL Marks in a way that is misleading, deceptive, or that disparages the project or its users.
- Applying for or registering any trademark that incorporates or is confusingly similar to the GARL Marks.

## 4. Fair attribution phrasing (recommended)

Good:
- "Powered by **GARL Protocol** (self-hosted)."
- "Compatible with GARL-format receipts."
- "Uses the open-source GARL Receipt Action."

Avoid:
- "GARL Verified" on content that wasn't signed by the canonical registry.
- "Official GARL Registry" for anything other than `api.garl.ai`.

## 5. Enforcement

If you believe the GARL Marks are being misused, please open an issue or email `contact@garl.ai`. We prefer to resolve concerns through direct conversation; we reserve the right to seek removal of infringing use when necessary.

## 6. Questions

This policy will evolve as the project and its community grow. Open an issue if a use-case isn't covered — we'd rather grant permission early than surprise someone later.
